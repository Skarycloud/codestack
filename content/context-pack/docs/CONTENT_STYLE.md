# Content style

## Voice

[e.g. Clear, friendly, direct. We explain, we don't sell.]

## Rules

- Sentence case for headings and buttons.
- Buttons say what happens: "Create task", not "Submit".
- Errors say what happened and what to do next.
- No jargon; use terms from docs/GLOSSARY.md.
- [Punctuation rules, e.g. no exclamation marks in errors]

## Examples

| Situation | Do | Don't |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Empty list | "No tasks yet. Create your first one." | "Nothing here!" |
| Error | "Couldn't save. Check your connection and try again." | "Error 500" |
